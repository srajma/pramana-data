# K11 — what each catalogue entry is (round 2026-10j)

Sub-task of the integrator #137. The user (2026-11-01): *"isn't the Sayana bhasya a commentary? (is it even complete
btw? it looks like a single page …) Isn't the Kanva Samhita a recension of the SYV? I feel like a lot of these in
general should not be under the Veda category at all … have a look through all such sundry works — maybe not just in
the Veda section — with a lot more attention to detail"*; earlier (10-28) Maitrāyaṇīyaśākhā, Vāstusūkta, Vedabhāṣya,
Vaidikasaṅgraha, Prasugranmantrasūkta "strewn over the Veda section".

K9/K10 put every work *somewhere* in the tree; K11 asked of every generated entry **what it is**: the same work as
another entry (merge), a commentary (on which exact base, by which person), a recension or edition, a part of a larger
work, a text of another genre, or a garbled headword. The Veda and Vedāṅga were read entry by entry by the agent
(texts opened where an entry had one: openings, colophons, running heads); the rest of Literature (153k entries) was
triaged by Gemini 3.8 Flash, every flag then checked a second time against its candidate target, and only flags with
evidence were applied (§4). Everything is a replayable decision file (catalog/k11/), applied by scripts/k11/apply.py
(also at the end of scripts/k9/replay.sh); old paths stay aliases (catalog/moved/k11.tsv).

## 4. How the model's verdicts were kept honest

* **Triage** (Flash, 25 entries per call with their siblings and NCC/Aufrecht lines): 26.6k of 153k entries flagged
  (duplicate 11.8k, wrong base 4.1k, wrong genre 3.9k, part 3.5k, garbled 2.5k, generic title 0.9k).
* **Second look** (one call per flag, the entry + up to six candidate targets, "same title alone is not enough"):
  17.8k flags → merge 10.9k, comm 1.8k, part 2.4k, none 2.5k.
* **Evidence guards** (scripts/k11/rules.py `evidence`): a merge needs one section and a shared author (a 5-letter name
  prefix: Caturvijaya/Caturavijaya); a part or a re-based commentary needs its target named in the entry's own
  title/references/summary (or to be its ancestor); a liturgical text (stotra, kavaca, nāma, māhātmya, pūjā, mantra,
  nyāsa, sūkta) is never made a "part" of the tantra or purāṇa it claims to come from; a re-basing across sections
  needs the target's author in the references. Random samples of each action were read before and after the guards
  (first sample: merges of different authors, Nammāḻvār's Tiruvāymoḻi "into" a Rāmāyaṇa chapter, kavacas as parts of
  the Rudrayāmala — all excluded by the guards).
* **Garbled titles**: confirmed by a second batch call before `title_uncertain` (2,044 of 2,476).
* Merges are oriented so the generated entry joins the curated one (or the one with a text); two texts stay two
  editions (`rec`); a curated entry is never merged away (3 such lines held).
* Not applied, for review (docs/work-2026-10j/K11-review/): flags the second look did not settle or the guards
  refused (`unsettled.tsv`), all wrong-genre flags (`genre.tsv`, 3,854), unconfirmed garbled titles (`garbled.tsv`),
  held curated merges (`curated-merge.tsv`), book/headword mismatches (`book.tsv`).
