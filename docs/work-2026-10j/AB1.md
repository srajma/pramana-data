## 1. Works in scope by kind

**Work in scope**: every work of the Literature catalogue (`in.*`: texts and commentaries) that is not marked lost.
That is **168,531** works (2,399 lost works left out). The catalogue is Aufrecht's Catalogus Catalogorum, the New
Catalogus Catalogorum (NCC), 16 other reference works, the canons, and the library's own sources (R3, R4, K9–K13).
Inscriptions (`inp.`), foreign sources (`inf.`) and studies (`ins.`) are not counted.

**Kinds.** Each work goes to the first kind that fits:
(a) a scan of a printed edition is online; (b) no printed scan, but a manuscript scan is online; (c) a printed edition
exists and is not scanned online; (d) manuscripts only, with no scan online.

**What is counted, and what is estimated.**

| part | works | basis |
|---|---|---|
| text here, from an edition or e-text | 19,027 | counted (live library, `status` available/partial). Counted as (a), because the editions behind e-texts are, with few exceptions, scanned online. Not sampled |
| text here, typed from manuscripts by others (Muktabodha: IFP, Jammu, Srinagar) | 223 | counted, as (d) |
| edition found on archive.org (hunt `accept`), not yet read | 2,655 | counted, as (a) |
| red links (no text), in 4 strata | 146,626 | **sampled**: 120 works drawn at random (seed 20261103) and checked by hand |

The strata cross two questions: does a catalogue name a printed edition (`meta.editions`, the NCC's "Ptd."), and did
the archive.org hunt search the work (R3/R4)?

Each sampled work was looked up on:
- archive.org (full text and metadata), HathiTrust full view, Google Books, Indian Culture, DLI mirrors;
- e-text sites (GRETIL, sanskritdocuments, Muktabodha, Wikisource);
- manuscript libraries online (eGangotri on archive.org, Cambridge CUDL, Wellcome, Tübingen, RAS, the Tamil Digital Library, the NMM viewer);
- the catalogues' own manuscript lists.

The verdicts and evidence for each work are in AB1-tables.md §1. The strata are scaled to their sizes.

which matters for small counts. Shares are of the 168,531 works in scope.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| (a) printed, scanned online | 48,000 (36,000–60,000) | 28.4 % | 19,027 | 39.8 % (32–53 %) |
| (b) manuscripts only, scanned online | 2,600 (520–8,500) | 1.6 % | 0 | 0 % |

**Caveats.**
- (b) is small and uncertain, because 3 of 120 sampled works were found only in a manuscript scan. Many eGangotri scans are titled loosely, so (b) is probably an undercount.
- (d) is large, because most of the NCC's works are known from one or two manuscripts. In the sample, 65 of the 80 works with no edition named had nothing printed and nothing scanned.
- "Scanned online" means a scan a person can reach without asking. The ≈ 4.4 lakh NMM / Gyan Bharatam manuscripts that are "view only" are counted under (d).

## 4. OCR error

**Bake-offs** (quoted, not re-run):

| bake-off | report | pages |
|---|---|---|
| modern prints | [docs/ocr-bakeoff.md](../ocr-bakeoff.md) | 50 pages, 9 editions |
| old prints, lithographs and commentary pages | [docs/ocr-old-prints.md](../ocr-old-prints.md) | 36–41 pages |
| newer models | [U5](../work-2026-10d/U5.md) | |
| manuscripts | [M4](../work-2026-10h/M4.md) | 52 of the 69 pages aligned with an edition |

How error is measured:
- **Excess error** is a model's letter error minus the best model's on the same page. This removes the differences between editions.
- **Gross failure** is a page more than 20 points worse than the best.
- **Manuscript CER** is measured against the edition passage the leaf carries. The manuscript's own readings are put into the reference, and spelling conventions (anusvāra, doubled consonants) are folded.

### In the library

`scripts/ab1/ocr_check.py`:
- **Pages per model.** These come from `meta.ocr_engine` of every `in.*` document, as page counts. The library has 2,476,949 OCR pages:

  | model | share of pages |
  |---|---|
  | Gemini 3.8 Flash | 67.2 % |
  | Claude Haiku 5.5 | 14.8 % |
  | Gemini 3.7 Flash | 12.5 % |
  | Gemini 3.1 Pro | 4.7 % (re-reads) |
  | Gemini 3.6 Flash | 0.8 % |
  | Gemini 4 Argon | 0.04 % |

- **Sample.** 120 pages, seed 20261103. A work was drawn with probability proportional to its pages, then one of its pages
  with passages on it was drawn uniformly. The model for each page is the OCR page store's: a 3.1 Pro re-read replaces the bulk reading.
  5 page images could not be fetched from archive.org. Of the remaining pages, 110 had at least 50 letters on both sides.
- **"Our text"** is what a reader sees for the page: the passages placed wholly on it (`seg_page`), as stored after
  structure, clean-up and re-reads.
- **Reference.** Each page was re-read fresh by Claude Opus 5.5 and by Gemini 4 Argon, with the library's tagged prompt and the strict suffix.
- **Error** is the number of edits to turn our letters into the best-aligned stretch of a re-read (semi-global Levenshtein,
  letters only, one romanisation: `scripts/m4/bakeoff.norm`), divided by our letters. The re-read part matches the document's kind (@M or @C);
  the whole page body is used instead if it aligns better. The figure per page is the **closer** of the two re-reads,
  so one re-reader's slip is not counted as ours.
- **Reference noise.** On the same pages the two re-reads differ by a median of **0.7 %** (107 pages).
- Opus refused or stopped early on 4 pages; Argon covers them.

| model | sample | mean (95 % bootstrap) | median | pages > 20 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | 72 | 7.0 % (3.7–11.0) | 1.3 % | 7 |
| Claude Haiku 5.5 | 21 | 8.5 % (2.8–16.2) | 2.4 % | 3 |
| Gemini 3.7 Flash | 12 | 6.3 % (0.8–14.4) | 0.8 % | 2 |
| Gemini 3.1 Pro | 5 | 1.3 % (0.6–1.9) | 0.9 % | 0 |
| **all** | 110 | **7.0 % (4.4–10.1)** | **1.5 %** | 12 |

| page kind | sample | mean | median | pages > 20 % |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| main text | 62 | 3.7 % | 1.6 % | 2 |
| commentary | 47 | 11.4 % | 1.1 % | 10 |

**Reading the result.** The typical page agrees with the bake-offs: a median of 1.5 %, against an excess of ≈ 1.5–3.5 % for Flash.
The mean is carried by 12 gross pages, 10 of them commentary pages:
- **3 are the right text tied to the wrong scan page.** The text matches another page of the same book at under 10 % error, so the passage-to-page map is off, not the OCR.
- **The other 9** are mostly commentary pages. On them our text differs from the page's own bulk OCR (`found_err`, 15–38 %) about as much as it differs from the re-reads, so the passages placed on these pages are largely not what the page carries. The likely causes are a passage-to-page map that drifts inside long commentaries, or text and commentary split differently (the scan-ocr-tagging problem, wiki memories/scan-ocr-tagging). Letters misread are the minor cause.

Per page, see AB1-tables.md §2.

**Manuscripts in the library: none have been read yet.** The 223 manuscript works here were typed by others. The rate the
library would carry is M4's: Gemini 4 Argon at 26.6 % letter error overall, 1.8 % on clean Devanāgarī, 16.7 % on hard Devanāgarī,
and 24.8 % on Tamil palm leaf.

## 5. Machine-translation error

**Bake-offs**: [docs/mt-bakeoff.md](../mt-bakeoff.md) (24 passages in 7 languages, judges Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.1 Pro)
and [docs/mt-bakeoff-foreign.md](../mt-bakeoff-foreign.md) (17 passages).

### In the library

`scripts/ab1/mt_check.py`:
- **Passages per engine.** These come from `meta.mt_engine`, live:

  | engine | passages |
  |---|---|
  | Gemini 3.8 Flash | 2,845,076 (98.5 %) |
  | Claude Opus 5.5 (foreign roots) | 28,421 |
  | JLOR's Gemini 1.5 Flash / Claude Haiku (imported) | 15,948 |
  | Gemini 3.1 Pro | 242 |

- **Sample.** Each engine was sampled separately (seed 20261103): a document drawn with probability proportional to its passages, then one
  passage uniformly. That gives 120 / 25 / 15 passages. One Flash passage lacks a second verdict, so 119 are scored.
- **Source.** The passage at the same locator in the copy the translation was made from (memories/section-numbering).
- **Judges.** The bake-off's two, judging blind (no engine named): accuracy 1–10, and errors by category and severity.
- **Major error.** A mistranslation, omission or addition a reader would be misled by.

| engine | passages | sample | accuracy (95 %) | major, both judges (Wilson 95 %) | major, either judge |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Gemini 3.8 Flash | 2,845,076 | 119 | 8.5 (8.2–8.8) | 9 % (5–16) — 11 | 15 % — 18 |
| Claude Opus 5.5 | 28,421 | 25 | 8.7 (8.2–9.2) | 4 % (1–20) — 1 | 8 % — 2 |
| JLOR (imported) | 15,948 | 15 | 4.5 (3.4–5.7) | 60 % (36–80) — 9 | 80 % — 12 |
| **all, weighted by passages** | 2,889,687 | 159 | 8.5 | **9 % (5–16)** | 15 % |

**Notes on the result.**
- **Judges differ.** Fable is stricter (Flash 7.8) than Gemini 3.1 Pro (9.2).
- **Major errors by kind**, both judges, weighted: mistranslation 50 %, addition 17 %, omission 12 %.
- **By language** (Flash, major by both judges): Sanskrit 6 of 59, Classical Chinese 0 of 26, Pāli 0 of 13, Tibetan 2 of 8, Kannada 1 of 5, Tamil 1 of 4, Persian 1 of 2.
- **Garbled source.** 10 of the Flash passages had a source the judges called garbled (bad OCR), and 1 of those had a major error.
- **JLOR.** The imported JLOR translations are the weak point. They are 0.55 % of the passages, and are worth replacing with Flash.
