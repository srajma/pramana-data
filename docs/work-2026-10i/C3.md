# C3 — important commentaries, included properly and cleanly (round 2026-10i)

A reader: *"the corpus has no commentaries on the Arthaśāstra or the Nītivākyāmṛta"*. The user asked for a pass that
makes sure all important commentaries are in the library, properly and cleanly. C3 was done in two halves: the
integrator's survey and clean-up (#137, commits 5a9e90f4, c0b79c32), and C3b (this sub-task: hunt, reading, staging,
a class fix for commentaries the books name differently, checks).

## Summary

* **The Arthaśāstra** now has seven commentaries with text: the Śrīmūla (complete, 118k words), Bhaṭṭasvāmin's
  Pratipadapañcikā, Mādhavayajvan's Nayacandrikā, the Cāṇakyaṭīkā (now 30.8k words, from the five-commentary
  edition), and — new — **Śaṅkarārya's Jayamaṅgalā** and **Yogghama's Nītinirṇīti** (from the five-commentary
  edition's vol. 1 part 1). **The Nītivākyāmṛta** has its anonymous Sanskrit **ṭīkā** (Māṇikacandra 22, 1923;
  39.5k words). Jayamaṅgalā, Nītinirṇīti and the Nītivākyāmṛta ṭīkā are linked verse by verse to their bases (scratch build).
* Of the 3,091 printed commentaries the survey expects on 887 works, **190 got text in C3b** (88 of importance 1,
  97 of importance 2, 5 of importance 3), besides 123 staged before C3b but not yet live when the survey looked;
  1,012 now have text in all (699 + 123 + 190).
* The stage gained **647 works** over the 2026-10-26 one (450 of them commentaries, 11.6 M words), from 62,000 pages read.
* **The "304" class (comm_label):** commentaries whose book names them by their proper name while the catalogue
  calls them "Commentary of X" or "…ṭīkā" staged nothing. Fixed as a class (a model picks the book's label once,
  `comm_label` in scans.yaml): **136 of the 297** such hunt entries without a record before now stage text (2.5 M words).
* **Spend: $285.75** of the $300 cap (the shared key's free balance $740.67 at the end, above its $700 floor).
* **Not found:** 431 commentaries of importance 1 and 943 of importance 2 had no verified printed edition online;
  the 40 most important are ranked below, the rest in [C3-commentaries.tsv](C3-commentaries.tsv).

### Commentaries the book names otherwise (`comm_label`, the integrator's "304")

The 10i release found 304 accepted, fully read hunt commentaries that staged nothing. `scanocr.book_record` dropped
them as "the book prints other commentaries, not this one": the OCR labels each commentary block by the name printed
in the book (`@C=Ānandacandrikā`), and `_is_wanted` compared those names only with the catalogue's title and the
author's name — "Ujjvalanīlamaṇiṭīkā", "Commentary of Viśveśvara", "Marīcikākaumudī".

* `pramana/commlabel.py`: for every accepted commentary entry whose read pages carry named commentaries of which none
  is wanted (349), Gemini 3.8 Flash is asked once (cached; $0.65 in all) which label, if any, is the entry's — given
  the base, the commentator, the catalogue's titles, the book's title and the hunt's check notes, and the labels with
  line counts. The answer is stored as `comm_label` (a label, or `none`) and `scanocr._is_wanted` takes it as the
  decision. Two guards: one book's commentary goes to one entry (56 duplicate entries of one book gave a label up,
  e.g. the three Padārthamālā entries → Laugākṣi Bhāskara's), and a label that is the title of a sibling entry with
  text is that sibling's (24, mostly unmerged duplicates such as Rasamañjarī `!ananta` / `!anantasrama`).
* A labelled commentary is no longer subjected to the "locate the work in a collection" step: printed beside other
  commentaries on the same pages (Nāgeśa's and Mahādeva's vṛttis on the Sāṃkhyasūtra) it has no page range.
* `comm_label` set by hand fixes the Arthaśāstra's Jayamaṅgalā: the book prints Śaṅkarārya's Jayamaṅgalā (464 lines)
  beside Rājarājeśvara Śāstrī Draviḍa's modern Jayamaṅgalākroḍapatra (7,946 lines), and the title alone matched both.
* `scripts/c3/titles.py`: the proper names the model gave went into the catalogue — 45 nodes; a generic title is
  replaced (old one kept in `titles:`: "Commentary of Viśveśvara" → Samañjasā, "Ujjvalanīlamaṇiṭīkā" → Ānandacandrikā,
  "Meghadūtaṭīkā" → Cāritravardhinī …), otherwise the name is added to `titles:`. Ananta's "Marīcikākaumudī" was not
  replaced: Marīcikā is another name of his Vyaṅgyārthakaumudī, which the catalogue already has as `!anantasrama`
  (a duplicate to merge).
* Test: `tests/test_c3_commlabel.py` (Viśveśvara's Samañjasā in a book with Sukhāvabodhā; the Jayamaṅgalā; one entry
  per label; the collection exemption).

**(a) Result:** 161 of the 349 labelled; **136 of the 297 hunt entries without a record before now stage text**
(2,544,672 words; list in [C3-commlabel.tsv](C3-commlabel.tsv)). Of the rest, 137 have no label (none of the book's
commentaries is theirs — the model says so with its reason), of the 24 labelled ones still without text, 18 are refused by the
front check (e.g. Anubhūtisvarūpa's entry labelled "Ānandagiri": rightly), 2 have too little own text, 1 is not
wholly read and 3 make a record that the stage keeps under another entry of the same book.

### Staging

`PRAMANA_STAGE_WORKERS=1 MALLOC_ARENA_MAX=2 pramana stage archiveorg && pramana scans front --write && … stage
archiveorg && pramana check archiveorg`, then once more after the collection exemption: 6,828 records, 4.64 M
segments, `pramana check archiveorg` clean (the one placeholder parent — R3's "Raudrī" matched to a Kārikāvalī with
the Rāmarudrī — rejected by hand).

**(b) Locating works in multi-work books cost $44.42** (front checks $12.99) — 20 % of the pass, against R4's 3 %
estimate. Cause: `scanocr.locate_work` caches its answer under `path|item|prompt`, and the prompt is a table of
*every read page's* head and first lines. Any book that gained pages since its last locate (R4's books read to the
end here, volumes added) gets a new key and is located again, at $0.01–0.03 a book, ≈ 2,000 books. **Next round:**
key the cache on the work (path, item) and the pages it was located in, or keep the old answer when the new table
only adds pages after the located range.

**Regression found:** 62 accepted entries that staged text on 2026-10-26 no longer do ([C3-lost-records.tsv](C3-lost-records.tsv)):
57 works in multi-work books the new locate answer says are not there (mostly short stotras of 100–1,000 words;
the larger ones Nārāyaṇa Paṇḍita on the Kumārasambhava 27k, Viśvanātha on the Grahalāghava 16k, Śrīdhara on the
Bhāgavata-upapurāṇa 10k, the Laghupārāśarasmṛti 10k — some of those old records were wrong: the Aṣṭādaśasmṛtayaḥ book
does not print the Laghupārāśara), 3 now filled from another source, 1 refused by the front check, 1 not wholly read
(Smṛtimuktāphala, whose new volumes L3 added). The same will happen at the release's restage.
